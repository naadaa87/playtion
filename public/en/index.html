Playtion Gacha Shop Konkuk — 600+ capsule toy machines in Seoul

A capsule toy (gacha) shop with 600+ machines on the 1st floor of the Hanareum building near Konkuk University Station in Seoul. How to get there, what's inside, prices per spin, card payment, and tips for first-time visitors.

English guide한국어日本語

Playtion Gacha Shop Konkuk is a capsule toy store with more than 600 gacha machines, run by Playtion, a Seoul play brand. It sits on the 1st floor of the Konkuk Hanareum building, a short walk from Konkuk University Station. No reservation is needed — just walk in, look around, and spin.

How to find us

Head to the Konkuk University Station area and look for a red exterior and a yellow sign. The shop is on the ground floor of the Konkuk Hanareum building. If you take a taxi, show the driver the Korean address above.

What's inside

The shop is divided into four zones so you can browse without getting lost.

You'll also find an opening table for unboxing your capsules, a photo spot made for your new favorites, and a capsule return box for empty capsules and paper inserts.

How to play gacha

  1. Walk around first. With hundreds of machines, it's worth one full lap before you spin.
  2. Read the label. Each machine shows the series name, the price per spin, and pictures of every item in the lineup.
  3. Pay. Most machines take cards or mobile payment. Follow the sticker on the machine.
  4. Turn the handle. One capsule comes out each time. You can't choose which item you get — that's the fun of gacha.
  5. Open it at the opening table so tiny parts don't fall on the floor.
  6. Return the empty capsule to the return box, keeping the paper separate.

Collecting a full set takes more spins than you might think. If a series has 5 items in equal numbers, it takes about 11 spins on average to get all five. Set a budget before you start, and trade duplicates with friends.

Good to know

  • Payment: Machines accept cards and mobile payment. Whether a specific foreign card works can vary by machine, so bring a backup card.
  • If a machine takes your money but nothing comes out, please don't shake it. Note the machine number and the time, and contact us at 1544-3523 (Korean) or through our help page.
  • Small parts: Many capsule toys contain tiny parts. Check the age label if you visit with young children.
  • Points: Korean visitors with a Playtion PASS can check in at the entrance QR code to collect points. It's optional.

After the gacha shop

Playtion also runs private game party rooms in Sinchon and near Konkuk Univ. Station. A group can book a whole room for a few hours or overnight, with board games, console games, and karaoke at the Sinchon branches. Booking is through Naver (Korean). See the party room page for details.

FAQ

Do I need to speak Korean?

No. The machines are self-service, and each label shows pictures of the items and the price.

Can I pay with a foreign credit card?

Machines take card and mobile payment, but acceptance of a specific foreign card can vary by machine. Bring a second card just in case.

Can I choose the item I want?

No. Gacha is random by design. If you get a duplicate, try trading with friends.

Do I need a reservation?

No. Just walk in.
